The cone penetration test (CPT) is the workhorse of modern site investigation — a steel cone pushed steadily into the ground while measuring the resistance it feels. From those simple measurements flows a remarkable amount of engineering knowledge: soil stratigraphy, strength and stiffness, and increasingly the direct design of foundations. CPT-based site characterisation and CPT-based design methods are central themes of my own research, and I use this section to share the fundamentals, the interpretation methods, and their application to foundation design.
