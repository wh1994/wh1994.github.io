Shaded: surrogate 90% (light) and 50% (dark) prediction intervals, calibrated to exact coverage on held-out sands. Lines: empirical correlations.

Surrogate: gradient-boosting quantile model (5th–95th percentile), monotone in e0 and p′, with conformal interval calibration; details in the accompanying article. Empirical correlations as published: Hardin & Richart (1963); Oztoprak & Bolton (2013); Wang (2022); Wichtmann & Triantafyllidis (2009); proposed equation (this study). Applicability: clean sands and gravels, p′ ≈ 15–590 kPa, e0 0.20–1.50, Cu 1–133, D50 0.10–70 mm. Predictions outside the training domain are flagged. No warranty — engineering judgement required.
